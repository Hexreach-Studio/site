◈Arcane Extraction

A single-player PvE extraction shooter about one idea: every spell is built, not picked. Socket a Frost aspect into a Bolt tome and you have an ice dart — socket it into a Wall and you raise a glacier.

Build, don't pick

8 elements × 10 tome forms × free-form sigils, wands and runes — hundreds of distinct spells from one crafting system.

A world that reacts

Fire detonates toxic clouds. Lightning electrifies frozen slicks. Wind spreads wildfire. The reaction matrix is the game.

Mobility has a price

Levitation and casting share one mana pool. Every second in the air is damage you can't deal on the ground.

Extraction with a conscience

Whatever you carry is lost on death. Whatever you extract is yours — and attuned gear survives even the worst run.

Enemies with your arsenal

Revenant Mages fight with the same spell system you do — and drop their actual builds when they fall.

The Storm Surge

Every raid is on a timer. Surge waves close the map — reach a Waygate before the frontier swallows your haul.

◈The Frontier

Drop into 25-minute raids across a four-province frontier — the Whispering Wilds, the Forgotten Coast, the Frozen Reaches, the Scorched Crater — and a drowned tidal marsh beyond the second gate.

Hexreach Studio

An independent game studio founded by a solo developer, building systems-dense dark fantasy with modern tooling — AI-assisted worldcraft, procedural audio, and an extraction loop tuned for one player first.
